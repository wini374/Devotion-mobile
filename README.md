DEVOTION

Every ending is a choice. Every choice leaves something behind.

《DEVOTION》是一款以終端機介面、文字敘事與解密探索為核心的互動式網頁故事，探討犧牲、記憶、執念，以及拯救世界所必須付出的代價。

在一個被「顏色」劃分的世界裡，不同陣營長年彼此敵視，卻有著一個共同的敵人——那位高踞基岩王座、以殘酷手段維持統治的神祕管理員 Null。

玩家將扮演一名倖存的反抗軍前任參謀，受託潛入管理員的底層系統，破解加密訊息、調查被刻意掩埋的紀錄，並尋找終結其統治的方法。

然而，隨著一道道權限被突破，隱藏在系統深處的真相也逐漸浮現。

那些被視為理所當然的仇恨、那些無法解釋的殘酷行為，以及那個始終不曾為自己辯解的暴君，似乎都隱藏著不為人知的秘密。

當過去的記憶與眼前的現實逐漸交錯，玩家將不得不重新審視自己所相信的一切。

如果你一直以來所憎恨的敵人，並不是你所認識的模樣，你還能毫不猶豫地按下終止鍵嗎？

有些真相，注定要付出代價才能知曉。

而有些選擇，一旦做出，就再也無法挽回。

DEVOTION is a browser-based interactive narrative told through a terminal-style interface, encrypted messages, and hidden records.

In a world divided by colors, rival factions have long been trapped in conflict, united only by their hatred of a single figure: Null, a ruthless administrator who rules from his throne of bedrock.

Players assume the role of a surviving resistance officer tasked with infiltrating the administrator’s system, uncovering classified records, and finding a way to end his reign.

Yet as forgotten memories and buried secrets begin to surface, the truth behind the tyrant’s actions grows increasingly uncertain.

What once seemed like a simple struggle between good and evil slowly becomes something far more complicated.

And when the truth is finally revealed, one question remains:

If the person you have spent years fighting is not who you believed him to be, will you still have the courage to press the final key?

Some truths come at a cost.

And some choices can never be undone.
